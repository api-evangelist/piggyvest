Sukuk (commonly called “Sukuk bonds”) are Sharia-compliant documents that serve as proof that you own a slice of a real asset (investment certificates) that give you part-ownership of a physical asset, such as a road or a building. Instead of paying interest like a conventional bond, they pay you rent or a share of profit. […]
